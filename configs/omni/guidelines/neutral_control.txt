A photograph is a two-dimensional record of light captured through a lens onto a sensor or film.
Composition: a picture may be framed in landscape or portrait orientation, and its subject may be centered or placed off-center.
Exposure: an image can be bright or dark overall, and its contrast may be high or low depending on the lighting of the scene.
Texture: surfaces in a scene can appear smooth, grainy, striped or patterned, and their tones range from black through grey to white.
Edges: objects are separated by sharp or soft transitions, and shadows or reflections can change how those transitions look.
Common descriptions mention the time of day, the weather, the colors present and the distance between the camera and the scene.
The border of a picture is where the recorded scene ends; margins, watermarks and captions are not part of the photographed content.
